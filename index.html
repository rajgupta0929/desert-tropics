The slow-burn itinerary

One big highlight, one good meal, and one easy evening at a time.

Dallas → Vegas → Utah → Vegas

Day 01 · Arrive

Dallas → Las Vegas

Fly in, collect the rental car, and ease into the trip.

  • Hotel pool reset
  • Bellagio fountains + LINQ stroll
  • Stock up on groceries and snacks
Sleep: Las Vegas
Day 02 · Explore

Classic Vegas, family-style

Keep the morning lazy, then enjoy the Strip’s free sights.

  • Bellagio Conservatory
  • Venetian canals
  • Optional discounted attraction
Sleep: Las Vegas
Day 03 · Day trip

Hoover Dam + Boulder City

Big engineering, wide-open views, and an unhurried lunch stop.

  • Dam overlooks and memorial bridge
  • Optional guided dam tour
  • Return for pool time
Sleep: Las Vegas
Day 04 · Road day

Valley of Fire → Springdale

Trade neon for glowing red sandstone and a scenic drive into Utah.

  • Atlatl Rock + Mouse’s Tank
  • Pack a picnic lunch
  • Check in near Zion
Sleep: Springdale or Hurricane
Day 05 · Wander

Zion National Park

Use the canyon shuttle and choose gentle trails at your own pace.

  • Riverside Walk
  • Pa’rus Trail
  • Picnic + afternoon swim
Sleep: Springdale or Hurricane
Day 06 · Viewpoints

Bryce Canyon → Kanab

Hoodoos, sunrise colors, and only as much hiking as feels fun.

  • Sunrise + Sunset Points
  • Bryce Amphitheater
  • Optional short loop hike
Sleep: Kanab
Day 07 · Grand finale

Grand Canyon → Las Vegas

An early start earns the family an unforgettable South Rim day.

  • Mather + Yavapai Points
  • Visitor Center
  • Drive back to Vegas
Sleep: Las Vegas
Day 08 · Depart

Red Rock + home

Finish with a scenic loop, an easy lunch, and your flight to Dallas.

  • 13-mile scenic drive
  • Short family trail
  • Return rental car
Fly home from Las Vegas

Travel tips

Small choices that keep this trip relaxed.

  • 💧Carry more water than you think.Desert heat makes short trails feel long.
  • 🎟️Reserve ahead.Check Zion shuttles, Red Rock timed entry, and park passes.
  • 🥪Pack lunch most days.Picnic meals save money and time at every park.
  • 🚗Protect the easy days.The Grand Canyon return is the longest driving day.
  • 🌤️Travel spring or fall.Skip peak summer heat and winter road surprises.
